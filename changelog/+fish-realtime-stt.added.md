- Added `FishAudioSTTService` for Fish Audio's realtime speech-to-text (`transcribe-stream-1`, and `transcribe-stream-2` with `text_format`). Fish Audio decides when the user's turn ends and tells it from noise and other voices, so a pipeline needs no local VAD: the service recommends `ExternalUserTurnStrategies` (or eager ones with `enable_eager_end_of_turn`), starts a turn once it has words, and pushes VAD speaking frames placed at the real start and end of speech so latency observers work without a VAD. With `vad_force_turn_endpoint=True` the pipeline's own turn strategies decide instead. The bot's replies are sent as conversation context, and a session the server ends is replaced.
